COMPANY FAQ

Company Name: TechStore

ABOUT THE COMPANY

TechStore is an online e-commerce company that sells
electronics, accessories and consumer technology products.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customer support is available Monday to Saturday from
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Customers can contact support through:
Email: support@techstore.example
Phone: +91-1800-000-000

ORDERS

Customers can check their order status from the
"My Orders" section of their account.

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not
be available.

RETURNS

Eligible products can be returned within 30 days of
delivery.

Products must be unused and in acceptable condition
for return eligibility.

REFUNDS

Approved refunds are generally processed within
5-7 business days.

The actual time for the refund to appear may depend
on the customer's payment provider.

PAYMENTS

TechStore accepts:

- UPI
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- Net banking

For payment-related issues, customers should contact
customer support.
