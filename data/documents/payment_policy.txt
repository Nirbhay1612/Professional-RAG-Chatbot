TECHSTORE PAYMENT POLICY

SUPPORTED PAYMENT METHODS

Customers can pay using:

- UPI
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- Net banking

PAYMENT FAILURE

If a payment fails but the amount has been deducted,
customers should wait for the payment status to update.

If the amount is not automatically reversed, customers
should contact their payment provider and TechStore
support.

PAYMENT SECURITY

Customers should never share their OTP, PIN, password
or other confidential payment information with anyone.

ORDER CONFIRMATION

An order confirmation is generated after successful
payment and order creation.

CASH ON DELIVERY

Cash on Delivery availability may depend on the product
and delivery location.
