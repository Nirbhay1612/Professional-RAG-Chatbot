TECHSTORE REFUND POLICY

REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Customers may be eligible for a refund when:

- The product is defective.
- The wrong product was delivered.
- The product was damaged during delivery.
- The return request meets the company's return policy.

REFUND PROCESS

1. Customer submits a return request.
2. The request is reviewed.
3. The product may be inspected after return.
4. If approved, the refund is initiated.

REFUND TIME

Approved refunds are generally processed within
5-7 business days.

PAYMENT METHOD

Refunds are generally sent back through the original
payment method, subject to the payment provider's
processing rules.

NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS

Certain products may not be eligible for return or
refund based on product-specific conditions.
