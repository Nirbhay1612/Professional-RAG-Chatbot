TECHSTORE SHIPPING POLICY

STANDARD SHIPPING

Standard shipping generally takes 3-7 business days.

EXPRESS SHIPPING

Express shipping may take 1-3 business days where
the service is available.

SHIPPING CHARGES

Shipping charges depend on the product, delivery
location and selected shipping method.

ORDER TRACKING

Customers can track their orders from the "My Orders"
section.

After shipment, customers may also receive tracking
information through their registered contact details.

DELIVERY DELAYS

Delivery times may be affected by weather, holidays,
logistics issues or other circumstances outside the
company's control.

ADDRESS CHANGES

Customers should contact support as soon as possible
if they need to change their delivery address.

Address changes may not be possible after shipment.
